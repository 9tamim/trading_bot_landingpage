Definition

What is Pipwright?

Pipwright is a cloud platform that runs six AI-driven forex trading bots on your broker account through MetaTrader 5, cTrader or TradingView webhooks. Each bot targets one trading style, such as trend following or London-open scalping, and applies fixed risk rules, spread filters and a news blackout before every order. It connects with trade-only permissions, so it cannot withdraw your funds.

How it trades

  • Enters in the direction of the higher-timeframe trend after a pullback to the 20 EMA.

How it trades

  • Ranks pairs by interest rate gap and swap cost, then filters by trend.

Known limits

  • Carry trades can lose sharply in a risk-off move.
  • Overnight swap changes with your broker and with central bank decisions.
  • Slowest bot, so the sample of trades is small.

Risk overlay

Hedge Guard

Sits on top of your other bots. It limits total exposure to one currency and trims positions when drawdown rises.

Timeframe
Account level
Holding time
Runs continuously
Sessions
Always on
Reads
Net exposure by currency, correlation between open trades, equity drawdown
Safeguards
Caps net exposure per currency, reduces size when drawdown passes your limit, can flatten all accounts

How it trades

  • Adds up exposure across every bot, so three long EUR trades are counted as one large position.
  • Blocks or shrinks new orders that would breach your currency cap.
  • Cuts position size in steps as drawdown grows, then restores it after recovery.

Known limits

  • It lowers risk, and it can also reduce profit.
  • It places no trades by itself.
  • Hedging is restricted for some account types, so check your broker.

Inside every bot

Four layers sit between a signal and your account.

A signal never becomes an order on its own. It passes through execution checks and risk limits first, and every decision is written to your journal.

Signal

Regime classifier
Labels each pair as trending, ranging or volatile on every new candle, so a bot only trades the conditions it was built for.
Headline reader
Scores central bank statements and data releases for direction and surprise, and passes the score to the bots that use it.

Execution

Spread and slippage guard
Holds an order when the live spread is wider than the bot's ceiling, and cancels fills that drift past your slippage cap.
Cloud execution
Bots run on our servers, so there is no VPS to rent and no terminal to leave open overnight.

Risk

Fixed fractional sizing
Lot size comes from your risk per trade and the stop distance, the same maths as the calculator below.
Kill switch and loss caps
Daily, weekly and equity limits pause every bot at once. One button closes all open positions.

Operations

Backtester and paper mode
Replay a bot on historical candles, then run it forward on live prices without risking money.
Trade journal
Each order stores the signal, regime, spread and the reason it was taken or skipped. Export it as CSV.

Questions

Frequently asked questions about AI forex bots.

What is an AI forex trading bot?

An AI forex trading bot is software that analyses currency prices and news, decides when to buy or sell, and sends orders to your broker without manual clicks. Pipwright bots use machine learning to classify market conditions and read headlines, and fixed rules to size trades and limit losses.

Can an AI forex bot guarantee profit?

No. No bot can guarantee profit, and forex trading on margin can lose more than your deposit. Pipwright does not publish promised returns. You can test each bot on historical data and on paper money to see how it behaves before you risk anything.

Which platforms does Pipwright work with?

Pipwright connects to MetaTrader 5 and cTrader accounts, and accepts TradingView alerts through a webhook. If your broker offers one of those platforms, the bots can usually trade it.

Can Pipwright withdraw money from my account?

No. You connect with trade-only permissions. Pipwright can place, modify and close orders, but it has no ability to withdraw or transfer funds. Your money stays with your broker.

Do I need a VPS to run the bots?

No. The bots run on Pipwright servers, so your computer can be off. You only need an account at a supported broker.

How does the risk management work?

Each trade is sized from a fixed percentage of equity and the stop distance. A spread guard blocks orders when costs jump, a news blackout pauses trading around scheduled releases, daily and weekly loss caps pause every bot, and a kill switch closes all positions.

Can I try it without risking money?

Yes. The Starter plan is free and runs in paper mode on live prices with simulated funds. You can also backtest on historical data. Moving to a live account is a separate step.

What is the best forex trading session for a bot?

It depends on the strategy. Trend and scalping bots favour the London session and the London and New York overlap (12:00 to 16:00 UTC) because liquidity is deepest. Range bots suit the quieter Sydney and Tokyo hours. Each Pipwright bot is scheduled for its own sessions.

Is automated forex trading legal where I live?

Rules vary by country. Some regions restrict leveraged forex for retail clients, and some brokers do not accept residents of certain countries. Check your local regulator and your broker before going live. Pipwright does not give legal or tax advice.

Which currency pairs are supported?

Pro and Desk support 28 major and minor pairs, such as EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY and AUD/USD. Starter paper mode supports three pairs.
